# JOSHUA V12: independent tactical verification and graph-evolution policy

**Study date:** 17 November 2026. **Status:** C++ source modified, Linux x86-64 binary rebuilt, authentic original paceval runtime exercised. **No new Stockfish strength result claimed.**

## 1. Why this experiment was undertaken

The most recent user-supplied `joshua_autoplay(2).zip` recorded two complete Stockfish 16 games. JOSHUA played with Model V4 and adaptive graph version 0. JOSHUA lost as Black after 35 halfmoves and as White after 24 halfmoves. The sample has no evidence of a newly promoted graph, and its size is insufficient for a strength estimate. The previous public V10 sample of 250 games remains historical and must not be added to these two as a single V12 strength evaluation.

Two critical decisions in the new sample were Black's `...d6` (game 1, ply 6) and White's `c4` (game 2, ply 9). They were flagged by the logged Stockfish analysis as substantial *heuristic* expected-score regressions, not by a formal chess proof. The third replayed case is White's `Qxd7+` (game 2, ply 15). The exact FEN strings are preserved in `results/v12_critical_positions.csv`.

## 2. Newly implemented search verification

The root search first evaluates every legal move to the requested nominal depth (normally 2). It computes the difference between the top two *white-oriented* scores in centipawns, without mixing score signs across turns. When the absolute difference is at most `--verify-margin-cp` and `--verify-extra-depth` is positive, JOSHUA immediately searches **every legal root move again** at the deeper horizon. It does not simply apply a score bonus to the originally chosen move. The final root minimax reduction still runs through original paceval graph handles; the neural V4 and adaptive scores remain genuine paceval evaluations.

This is a **search-policy change**, not a new trained model. It is C++ orchestration over existing mathematical graph calculations. The verification rule is deterministic given an identical position, graph version and configuration. It does not accept move suggestions from Stockfish during play.

Commands for direct investigation:

```bash
./joshua --model 4 --depth 2 --quiescence 2 \
  --verify-margin-cp 25 --verify-extra-depth 1 \
  --fen 'rnbqkb1r/pppppppp/8/4P3/4n3/3P4/PPP2PPP/RNBQKBNR b KQkq - 0 3' \
  --graph-file config/active_graph.tsv --plies 1 --no-board --output research_game1_ply6
```

All three original and revised moves were generated from the *same* historical FEN inputs; the revised moves were executed by the compiled C++ engine with the supplied original paceval `.so`. ## 4. Adaptive mathematical graph proposals

The original graph file is still `stockfish_sessions/active_graph.tsv`. An actual evaluated graph is exported to `active_graph_expression.txt`, its terms to `active_graph_terms.csv`, and all registered graph formulas to `active_full_graph_catalog.csv`. The V12 candidate generator makes up to eight proposals by default (`--candidate-budget 8`), drawing on three distinct tactical *whitelisted* nonlinear templates plus linear alternatives. Candidate formulas are generated by the genuine original paceval parser during execution. There is no arbitrary code generation or dangerous expression evaluation from the TSV.

Candidates must still pass independent Stockfish position checks, separate holdout conditions, and fully adjudicated, color-paired challenger/champion games. Because the user upload has only two games at version 0, **no new version was promoted in this release**.

## 5. Better champion/challenger openings

The earlier V11 comparison used fixed, unusual single opening moves, including `f3` and `g4`. V12 instead uses six predeclared *four-ply* openings with equal positions for the two reversed-color matches. These include standard King's Pawn, Queen's Pawn, English, Réti, fianchetto, and Sicilian beginnings. All 24 forced opening moves were accepted by the compiled C++ referee. Such positions are more representative but still do not guarantee a statistically reliable strength estimate.

The controller's `comparison_key()` now includes `verify_margin_cp` and `verify_extra_depth`: a depth-2 V11 series is not silently compared as if it were the same search configuration as V12 depth-2 plus conditional depth-3 verification. A color pair is only eligible for learning if the same graph-file SHA-256 and fixed engine/opponent configuration were used for both games.

## 6. What the referee and Stockfish separately do

The C++ referee accepts a six-field FEN and legal UCI move. It checks chess legality and applies the move to produce a verified next FEN, SAN and terminal status. It handles checkmate, stalemate, insufficient material, threefold claims and fifty-move claims under the documented match policy. It does **not** judge whether a legal move is strategically best.

Stockfish independently scores the position before and after Joshua's move at a fixed search-node budget. Its UCI scores form a bounded heuristic training signal, not a calibrated winning probability and not a formal adjudication. An accepted mathematical candidate may improve these local scores and still fail the later game-level strength gate.

## 7. Verification record and limitations

- Linux ELF builds completed for `joshua`, `joshua_referee`, `joshua_selfplay`, and `joshua_long_graph`.
- Eight targeted CTest cases passed, including authentic paceval tactical-search replay, six legally replayed champion openings, multi-template proposal policy, prior V9/V10/V11 regression policies, and GitHub math checker negative tests.
- The genuine updated Joshua binary completed two bounded protocol-integration games with a **MOCK UCI peer**, producing ZIP archives, real paceval evidence, timing fields and color rotation. These games ended at an artificial six-ply limit and are **not Stockfish games**.
- The original paceval runtime was available from the private upload. Stockfish itself was not present in this execution environment. **No new full-strength Stockfish tournament was conducted here.**
- A longer additional whole-suite replay encountered an execution timeout; only independently completed individual results are reported as passed.
- The V12 root uncertainty test can trigger more expensive searches. A larger search does not prove a better move, and some deeply tactical or strategic errors require improved features, evaluation, or a much stronger search than depth 3.

## 8. Interpreting V12 versus V11

V12 provides verified improvements in **functionality, auditability and search consistency**. No improvement or deterioration in general chess strength has yet been established. To test that claim, freeze the graph and search policy, run complete paired games against the same Stockfish build with stable settings, then repeat with different openings and disjoint holdout games. Until such tests exist the adaptive graph remains version 0.

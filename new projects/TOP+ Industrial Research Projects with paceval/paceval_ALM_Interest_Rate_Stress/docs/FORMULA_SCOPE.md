# Mathematical scope, identities and decisions

**V1** is an illustrative continuously compounded base zero-rate curve plus only a parallel perturbation. **V2** adds tenor-dependent short/long shock factors. The base curve is $L+S(1-e^{-t/\tau})+H(t/\tau)e^{-t/\tau}$, where $t$ and $\tau$ are in years and $L,S,H$ are dimensionless annual zero rates. V2 adds $p+s e^{-t/4}+\ell(1-e^{-t/4})$. The resulting bond-equivalent PV of each fixed positive cash flow is $CF\exp(-rt)$, with $CF$ in EUR millions.

The discounted cash-flow liability is stored as a positive liability magnitude and subtracted from asset PV at the portfolio level. Asset and liability cashflow amounts are not signed input numbers. First derivative with respect to parallel stress is $-tPV$ and second is $t^2PV$. Duration here means PV-weighted mean maturity, not modified yield-to-maturity duration under discrete compounding. Cash-flow PV01 is the signed first-order derivative multiplied by 0.0001. No automatic derivative API is asserted; the derivatives are explicitly authored formulas and independently verified.

The host sums variable-length arrays for asset PV, liability PV, first and second rate derivatives, duration numerators and tenor sensitivities. Dedicated paceval `SUM_*` graphs compute final gap, mismatch, ratios, and a **research-only loss threshold of EUR 20 million**. The resulting `LOSS_GUARD` is not a regulatory capital limit or a bank permission to execute trades. An 81-point smooth stress map is built using a real paceval single-graph batch and is not an optimized portfolio strategy.

A Basel-prescribed IRRBB implementation would use currency-specific shock magnitudes, calibrated rate curves, behavioral cashflows, prepayments, optionality and supervisory methodologies, none of which are claimed here. Negative continuous zero rates are mathematically permitted in the research domain; their discount factors can exceed unity and should not be silently clipped to one.

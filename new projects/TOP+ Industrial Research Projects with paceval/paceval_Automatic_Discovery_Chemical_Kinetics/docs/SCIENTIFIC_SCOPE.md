# WP 27 | Scientific scope and study design

The study is a deterministic proof of concept for constrained finite-library rate-law selection. Its **ground truth is intentionally generated by the same known functional family** that the test search may examine. Discovery here means selecting a planted mathematical rate-law form from 1,000 predeclared alternatives, not inventing a previously unknown molecular reaction mechanism.

## Model

The modelled reaction has a stoichiometric vector $(-1,-1,+1)$ for a synthetic $A+B\rightarrow P$ event. Both the latent generator and candidate library use an Arrhenius temperature factor referenced to 310 K to avoid unnecessarily large or small direct prefactors. Inhibited and saturated variants are mathematical ansatz families, not verified molecular mechanisms.

The input concentrations are strictly positive, ensuring that non-integer powers and sensitivity factors are defined. The 1.6 mol L$^{-1}$ h$^{-1}$ guard is a hypothetical mathematical threshold, **not** a process safety limit. The RK4 concentration curves are isothermal, perfectly mixed zero-dimensional demonstrations.

## Study protocol

Candidate model fitting is limited to 512 training records; 256 validation records determine the selection with a small explicit complexity cost; 256 high-temperature holdout cases are untouched until final model selection. Exact candidate enumeration and observed rate construction are in the C++ program. Both selected frozen graph versions are independently validated. Full test reproduction must link an original paceval runtime.

## How this differs from recent research

Contemporary SISR and physics-informed symbolic regression algorithms can explore search spaces more flexibly and account for reaction-network stoichiometry, experimental error and implicit mechanisms. WP27 does not implement their genetic search, grammar search, discovery of unknown species, identifiability proofs, uncertainty posterior or cross-laboratory validation. It specifically demonstrates paceval as the mathematical execution layer within such a potential workflow.
